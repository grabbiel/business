Word Pool

A word search game where you pick the subject.

Type anything — sea creatures, Baroque composers, things in your kitchen — and get a grid built around it. Then find the words.

    Three ways to play

    From no pressure to full pressure.

    01

    Practice

    No clock, no score. Clear a grid and the next one appears. Play for a minute or an hour.

    02

    Timer

    One grid, one countdown. Finish it and you'll see your time and whether it beat your best.

    03

    Multiplayer

    Get matched with another player on the same grid. First to find every word wins.

    Any subject

    The grid is built around whatever you're interested in.

    Most word search games ship with a fixed set of puzzles and you work through them. This one generates the words from a topic you type, so the grids don't run out and they're about things you actually care about.
